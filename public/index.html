Share your experience

Dealt with Khurana Homescapes in Punjabi Bagh, Meera Bagh, Paschim Vihar or elsewhere in West Delhi? Tell us what happened, in your own words. You can post anonymously. Contact details are optional and are never published.

Your submission is sent to this site's own server and nowhere else. No account is needed and your IP address is not stored with your review.

Guidelines

  • Only genuine experiences from real buyers or customers. Do not post on behalf of someone else, and do not post made-up or copied reviews.
  • Describe facts as you experienced them: what was promised, what was paid, what was delivered, and when. Keep documents safe and do not post private information about other people (phone numbers, addresses, ID numbers).
  • No abuse, threats or hate. Opinions are welcome; unsupported accusations are not.
  • Every submission is read by a moderator before it appears. Reviews that break these guidelines are not published.
  • Anyone named in a review who wishes to respond or point out a correction can write to khuranahomescape@gmail.com. Fair responses can be published alongside the review.
